# Effects library

Owner column: D = the design side implements (static or structural); M = the Motion team implements (anything time- or input-driven). Design only specifies M items in the MOTION HANDOFF of the visual brief. Cost: L/M/H = paint/composite cost on a mid-range phone (judgment, UNVERIFIED unless cited). Every effect is opt-in through the visual brief "EFFECTS" field with its parameters; none is a default. [S#] tags point to sources.md.
